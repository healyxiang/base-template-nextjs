## Make it yours

Start with your brand, your audience, and one useful feature. Update the central site configuration with your name, navigation, and credit packs.

## Connect your services

Create a separate Supabase project, configure authentication, and map your Waffo products to the credit packs. Keep test and production environments separate.

## Publish with confidence

Replace the example policies and support details. Check your translated pages, publish your content, and submit your public URLs to search engines.
